ChatGPT was used as an assistance tool during the development of this School Admission System.

The assistance included:


1. Troubleshooting development issues, including C# nullable reference warnings.
2. Understanding database queries and verifying seat availability calculations.
3. Planning and performing API, Docker, and automated test verification.
4. Reviewing project documentation requirements and identifying missing items.

## Human Review and Verification

- AI suggestions were reviewed before being applied.

- Docker services were started and verified.
- Automated tests were executed in Docker: 6 passed, 0 failed.

## Limitations

AI-generated suggestions may contain mistakes or assumptions. Suggestions were checked against the actual project behavior and corrected where necessary.
